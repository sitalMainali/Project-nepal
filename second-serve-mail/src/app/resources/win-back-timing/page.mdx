## Define "lapsed" for your venue

- A neighbourhood café and a special-occasion restaurant have very different visit rhythms.
- Look at your booking data: how long do regulars usually go between visits?
- A useful starting point is roughly double your typical gap between visits. <Placeholder>[ADD YOUR OWN DATA OR A CITED BENCHMARK]</Placeholder>

## A three-step win-back sequence

1. **The friendly nudge.** "We've missed you", plus what's new on the menu. No offer.
2. **The reason to return.** An event, a seasonal dish or a small, specific offer.
3. **The graceful goodbye.** Ask if they'd still like to hear from you. Remove them if not.

## Spacing and frequency

- Leave at least a week or two between steps.
- Pause win-backs for anyone who books in the meantime.
- Don't overlap win-backs with your monthly campaign in the same week.

## Email or SMS?

- Email for storytelling and menus.
- SMS for short, time-sensitive reasons to book. Use it sparingly.

## Measuring it honestly

- Track bookings or redemptions you can trace back to the message.
- Compare against a small group who didn't get the message, if your list is large enough.
