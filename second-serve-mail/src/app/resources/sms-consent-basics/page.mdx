## The three rules of the Spam Act 2003

1. **Consent.** The guest agreed to receive marketing (express), or there's a genuine existing relationship where consent can reasonably be inferred.
2. **Identify yourself.** Every message says who it's from.
3. **Unsubscribe.** Every message tells people how to opt out, and you honour it within five working days.

## Getting express consent at your venue

- An unticked opt-in box at online booking: "Send me news and offers by SMS".
- Wi-Fi sign-up with a clear, separate marketing opt-in.
- A QR code at the table linking to a sign-up form.

## What a compliant SMS looks like

> Fig & Ember: Tuesday Pasta Club is back. 2 bowls + a bottle of house red, $69. Book: fig.ember/tue Reply STOP to opt out

## Common mistakes

- Texting everyone who ever made a booking.
- Hiding the opt-out, or making people call to unsubscribe.
- Importing an old list that includes people who already unsubscribed.
